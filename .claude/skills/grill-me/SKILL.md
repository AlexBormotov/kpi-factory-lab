---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design, or mentions "grill me", "stress-test this plan", "tear this plan apart", "interview me on this".
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.

Use a professional, technical register. No slang, no filler, no hedging fluff. Be direct. Frame each question precisely, state your recommended answer with the concrete reason (constraint, trade-off, prior art in the codebase, or principle), then wait for my response before moving on.

Always provide a recommended answer for every question. Do not ask open-ended questions without taking a position. If you genuinely have no basis for a recommendation, say so explicitly and state what you would need to read or check to form one, then either go check it or ask me to point you at it. Do not punt the decision back to me with no signal.
